Classic Pizza in Fort Bragg

Located on Redwood Ave in downtown Fort Bragg, minutes from the Mendocino Coast and the Skunk Train.

Bernillo's is owner operated and each pizza is crafted with care. Wait times may vary during peak hours. Dine in or call (707) 964-9314 to order takeout.
